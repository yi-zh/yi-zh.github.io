Optimality Conditions for Mixed-Integer Nonlinear Programming (MINLP)

Optimal points in linear optimization problems are known to lie on the boundary of the feasible region, while solutions to continuous nonlinear optimization problems must satisfy the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions. However, mixed-integer nonlinear programming (MINLP) problems lack such straightforward and well-defined optimality conditions. In this work, we propose novel optimality conditions for MINLP and present an efficient implementation of new domain reduction techniques based on these conditions. These techniques have been integrated into the global MINLP solver BARON since 2018, resulting in significant speed-ups, as demonstrated by computational experiments across a broad collection of benchmark NLP and MINLP libraries.

Cite:
Zhang, Y., Sahinidis, N.V., Nohra, C. et al. Optimality-based domain reduction for inequality-constrained NLP and MINLP problems J Glob Optim 77, 425–454 (2020).

From this work, you will learn:
  • Bound propagation on gradients
  • Domain reduction
  • KKT conditions

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Online AGV Path Finding

Multi-agent pathfinding (MAPF) naturally arises in applications such as the pick-up and drop-off of parcels by automated guided vehicles (AGVs) in warehouses. Existing algorithms, such as Conflict-Based Search (CBS), Windowed Hierarchical Cooperative A* (WHCA), and other A* variants, are widely used to find shortest paths in various ways. However, in real-world environments, MAPF problems are dynamically generated and must be solved in real time. We propose a decentralized multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework combined with a multi-step ahead tree search (MATS) strategy to make efficient decisions. Through experiments on both numerical cases and real-world warehouse scenarios, our proposed MARL policy demonstrates better performance in problem scale, solution time, and length of paths than classical methods.

Cite:
Zhang, Y., Qian, Y., Yao, Y.C. et al. Learning to cooperate: Application of deep reinforcement learning for online AGV path finding . Proceedings of the 19th International Conference on autonomous agents and multiagent systems, 2077-2079 (2020).

From this work, you will learn:
  • Multi-agent path finding (MAPF)
  • Multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL)

Optimization in the Cloud for Large-Scale Problems

In many real-world applications, optimization models can scale to over 10 million variables with sparse coefficient matrices, as seen in logistics assignment problems. Traditional optimization solvers, which typically operate in standalone mode, often struggle to handle such memory-intensive cases. To address this challenge, we propose a distributed optimization framework that constructs and presolves models in a streaming mode. This framework decomposes large-scale optimization problems into sub-blocks and solves them in a distributed manner. It is particularly effective for problems with block-diagonal structures and ensures balanced computational loads across all nodes.

From this work, you will learn:
  • Modeling in streaming mode
  • Presolve and decompose a model in cloud
  • Distributed optimization
